# Screenshot Guide

For the IBM Cloud Native Full Stack Development Capstone Project, you need to submit the following screenshots:

1. **`about_page.png`**: Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:8000/about` and capture the About Us page.
2. **`contact_page.png`**: Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:8000/contact` and capture the Contact Us page.
3. **`react_registration.png`**: Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:8000/register` and capture the new registration form in React.
4. **`django_admin_login.png`**: Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin` and capture the login screen.
5. **`dealers_before_login.png`**: Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:8000/` before logging in, showing the top right "Login" button and dealers list.
6. **`dealers_after_login.png`**: Navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:8000/` after logging in, showing the username in the top right.
7. **`dealers_filtered.png`**: On the dealers page, use the state dropdown to filter by a specific state (e.g., Texas) and capture.
8. **`dealer_details.png`**: Click on a dealer to view their details (`/dealer/:id`) and capture the page showing reviews.
9. **`post_review_form.png`**: Click "Write a Review" and capture the form before submission.
10. **`review_posted.png`**: Submit the review and capture the dealer details page showing the newly posted review.
11. **`github_actions_success.png`**: Go to your GitHub repository -> Actions tab, and capture the green checkmark showing successful CI/CD pipeline run.
12. **`code_engine_deployment.png`**: Capture the IBM Code Engine dashboard showing your deployed application running successfully.
